The core trade-off you described

You said the net-net pay is roughly equal after CA tax + SF cost of living. The table above explains why: Meta's higher headline TC gets clawed back by CA income tax (~$30–40K/yr at this income) and ~32% higher cost of living. You are essentially paying for SF lifestyle and Meta's brand with ~the same take-home — and absorbing more risk & stress to do it.

So the decision cannot be made on money. It has to be made on: (1) career trajectory, (2) life fit / stress tolerance, (3) optionality.

  • Bay Area network density — best AI/ML talent gravity in the world
  • WhatsApp Business is a Meta growth bet (~$138B est. valuation, 50M+ businesses, 175M daily conversations)
  • Meta brand on resume opens FAANG-tier doors faster than Amazon-only path
  • Working on a consumer-scale messaging + commerce surface = unique data + ML problems

  • Meta PSC (now "Checkpoint") explicitly targets bottom 7–10% — Meta is doing 8K layoffs May 20 2026 with more in H2
  • WLB rating 2.5 vs Amazon's 3.1; "Recommend to a friend" 49% vs 78%
  • You already flagged anxiety as higher at Meta — that compounds over time, not lessens
  • Net-in-hand is roughly flat, so you're trading certainty for risk with no premium
  • Senior Scientist (L6) → IC5 may be a lateral or slight down-level depending on scope; Amazon Principal (L7) is a clearer path internally than Meta E6
  • You'd lose WA's 0% income tax — a structural advantage worth ~$30–40K/yr

The "30% chance of bad" on Meta is the headline number. It is much higher than the equivalent risk at Amazon today.

The Alternatives You Should Weigh

Option 1 — Amazon internal transfer to Bay Area

Recommended to explore first

Amazon has a real Bay Area presence (Sunnyvale, San Francisco, East Palo Alto, Lab126, AGI). An internal L6 transfer keeps your tenure clock, your stock vest schedule, and your manager relationships — but gives you the Bay Area life you said you want. Downsides: you still pay CA tax, but you don't take Meta-tier layoff risk.

Action: post in internal mobility, talk to Amazon Bay Area science orgs (AGI, Alexa AI, AWS AI, Lab126). 4–8 week process.

Option 2 — Keep interviewing at peers (Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, Apple, Netflix)

High value — costs you nothing to run

Right now Meta is your only outside offer. That means you have zero leverage and one data point. Spinning up 2–3 more loops in parallel either:

  • Gets you a higher Meta counter (Meta will match competing offers)
  • Surfaces a better fit (Google/Apple WLB tends to score higher than Meta's; Anthropic / OpenAI for ML upside)
  • Lets you say "no" to Meta from a position of strength, not fear

Time cost: ~6–10 weeks. Realistic uplift: a real second offer plus +$50–150K negotiation room on whichever you take.

Option 3 — Take Meta as planned

Only if you can answer "yes" to all three

  • You've stress-tested how you handle Meta-style stack rank for 24+ months
  • You have ~9 months of expenses banked in case of layoff
  • You genuinely want SF lifestyle + network for non-career reasons (founding a startup, partner's job, etc.) — and would still want it if comp were the same at both

Option 4 — Use this offer to negotiate at Amazon

Low risk, often underused

An external Meta IC5 offer is a credible threat. Bring it to your Amazon manager + skip with a clear ask: an out-of-cycle equity refresh, an L7 promo plan with milestones, and/or an internal transfer to the Bay Area. Worst case: they say no and you're back to today's choice.

Recommendation

Don't take the Meta offer as-is. Don't simply stay either.

Given everything you told me — equal net-in-hand, higher anxiety at Meta, and a personal interest in the Bay Area — taking Meta IC5 is a negative-expected-value move on a risk-adjusted basis. You'd be trading a known, lower-stress, tax-advantaged role for a higher-stress role at a company actively running performance-based layoffs in 2026, for no real cash premium.

The 90-day playbook I'd run if I were you

  1. Weeks 1–2: Initiate Amazon internal mobility conversations for Bay Area orgs (AGI, AWS AI, Lab126). Tell your manager you're exploring — not threatening.
  2. Weeks 1–6: Open 2–3 parallel interview loops (Google, Apple, Anthropic). Pick teams with better WLB signals than Meta's.
  3. Weeks 4–6: Use the live Meta offer as leverage — either get an Amazon equity refresh / L7 plan, or a stronger competing offer.
  4. Weeks 6–10: Re-decide with at least 2 outside offers + 1 internal counter on the table. Now the choice is informed.
  5. Decline Meta now if: the offer expires before you can run this. Walking away from a flat-net-pay, higher-stress role is a non-event.

Decision rule, in one sentence

Take Meta only if (a) net comp clears Amazon by ≥ $60K/yr after CA tax + COL, or (b) the team/scope is so strategic (e.g., direct reports, product ownership, AI infra leadership) that it's effectively a promo. Neither is true based on what you described — so the answer is no, with a plan.
